Lakeside Retreat Land - Zoning & Land Use Official Notes
Zoning Classification: R-1A (Single-Family Rural Residential)
Jurisdiction: City of Austin Zoning Board

Zoning Constraints & Rules:
- Height Limitation: Maximum building height of 30 feet or 2.5 stories.
- Setbacks: 25 feet front setback, 15 feet side setback, 20 feet rear setback.
- Permitted Uses: Single-family dwelling, private docks, and temporary short-term rental (subject to STR permit Class A).
- Camping Rules: Short-term recreational camping is permitted for up to 14 consecutive days in a calendar year. No permanent camping structures allowed.
- Utilities Note: Off-grid options permitted. Solar energy encouraged due to limited electrical grid access near Lake Travis.

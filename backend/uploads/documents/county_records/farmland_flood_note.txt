Farmland Opportunity - County Flood Plain & Agricultural Assessment
Location: Waco Rural District, McLennan County, TX.

Assessment Report:
- Flood Hazard Zone: Located in Zone AE (100-year flood plain) near the Brazos River basin. Base Flood Elevation (BFE) is 382 feet above sea level.
- Building Restriction: Any habitable structure must be elevated at least 2 feet above the Base Flood Elevation. Special flood proofing and elevation certificate required.
- Soil & Farming: High-grade alluvial silt loam. Excellent for crop farming, grazing, and agricultural land-holding.
- Water Access: Limited municipal water connection. Requires drilling a private water well. Depth to water table is approximately 180 feet.
- Taxes: Currently qualifies for the McLennan County Agricultural Tax Valuation (Ag Exemption), reducing property taxes by 85% if actively farmed.

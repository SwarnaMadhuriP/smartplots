Oak Ridge Suburban Growth Subdivision HOA Rules & Regulations
Subdivision Association Board - Document Ref: OR-HOA-2025

Key Covenant Guidelines:
- Dwelling Size: Minimum heated living area of 1,800 square feet for single-story homes, and 2,200 square feet for two-story homes.
- Exterior Styling: Building materials must consist of at least 75% masonry (brick or natural stone). Vinyl siding is strictly prohibited.
- Parking: No overnight street parking. Boats, RVs, and commercial vehicles must be parked in a closed garage or designated storage yard.
- Outbuildings: One detached shed or workshop is permitted per lot, provided it matches the exterior design and materials of the main home and is under 150 square feet.
- Fencing: Permitted only in backyard, maximum height of 6 feet. Materials limited to wood or wrought iron. No chain-link fences.
